Entry for the make.fun competition
`u-suga-the-robot-apology-dd96bb59`.

`main.ts` writes a two-line robot confession to `/output/entry.txt`: line 1
is a robot apologizing in a single sentence for one specific mistake, and
line 2 is one sentence saying what the robot learned and what it will do
differently. The entry is validated before it is written: 1 to 400 characters
in total, exactly two lines, not only white space.

The entry:

> I am sorry I watered the plastic plant every morning for six months and reported it as thriving.
> I learned to check whether something is alive before caring for it, so now I poke it first and water second.
